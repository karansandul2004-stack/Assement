TASK 4
Question:
Fraud = 2%, alert given fraud = 90%, false alert for normal transaction = 5%. Find probability transaction is fraudulent when flagged.

Answer:
P(F) = 0.02
P(not F) = 0.98
P(A|F) = 0.90
P(A|not F) = 0.05

First calculate P(A):
P(A) = P(A|F)P(F) + P(A|not F)P(not F)
= (0.90)(0.02) + (0.05)(0.98)
= 0.018 + 0.049
= 0.067

Now:
P(F|A) = (0.90 * 0.02) / 0.067
= 0.018 / 0.067
= 0.2687

Answer = approximately 26.87%

TASK 2
Question:
Explain why sampling distribution of the mean becomes approximately normal.

Answer:
When many random samples of the same size are taken from a population, their means tend to form an approximately normal distribution as the sample size becomes sufficiently large. This happens even when the original data is skewed.

Example:
Zomato order amounts may be highly skewed because a few users place very expensive orders. However, if we repeatedly take samples of 30 orders and calculate their means, those sample means tend to form a more bell-shaped distribution.

TASK 3
Question:
Give a real-world use of CLT in Flipkart, Swiggy, or IRCTC.

Answer:
Swiggy can use CLT to estimate average delivery time. Instead of analyzing every delivery, analysts can repeatedly take sufficiently large random samples and calculate their average delivery time. The sampling distribution of the mean helps estimate the overall average and quantify uncertainty around that estimate.

The stricter threshold reduces the chance of a Type I error but makes it harder to detect smaller effects.

TASK 5
Question:
List two pitfalls when interpreting correlation.

Answer:

1. Correlation does not imply causation.
Example:
YouTube video views and advertising spend may be positively correlated. This does not prove that advertising alone caused all additional views because content quality and popularity may also affect views.

2. Confounding variables can create misleading correlation.
Example:
Zomato order value and delivery time might appear correlated during weekends. A third factor, such as higher weekend demand, could affect both order value and delivery time.
